## 5. IEEE Style Conventions Applied

For academic defense and peer-review audits, this bibliography complies with IEEE reference rules:
1. **Author Name Formatting:** Given name initials are placed before surnames (e.g., `P. He`, `J. Zhu`). For items with more than six authors, the lead author is listed followed by `et al.`.
2. **Title Punctuation:** Paper and article titles are enclosed in double quotes with commas inside the quotation marks (e.g., `"Drain: An online log parsing approach with fixed depth tree,"`).
3. **Publication Titles:** Conference proceedings and journals are set in italics using standard IEEE abbreviations (e.g., *Proc. IEEE Int. Conf. Web Serv. (ICWS)*, *Commun. ACM*).
4. **Volume and Issue Details:** Expressed cleanly as `vol. X, no. Y, pp. Z–W, Month Year`.
5. **Electronic Identifiers & DOIs:** DOIs are prefixed with `doi: 10.xxxx/...`; online electronic records are given with `[Online]. Available:` and the direct resolution link.
6. **Statutory & Legal Citations:** Legal instruments (CERT-In Directions, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023) are cited using the standard IEEE format for government statutes, including gazette notification details and issuing authorities.
